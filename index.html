A LETTER FROM AHMIE

For Inshaal,

I don't want this letter to be about holding you back, controlling your choices, or asking you to belong to me.

You have always been free to choose the person who makes your heart happy. If one day you choose someone else, I will respect that choice, even if it hurts.

But if life ever brings you back to me, from anywhere, at any time, I want you to know something simple: I would still open the door for you.

I can stay beside you for as long as you genuinely want me there. But if you ever decide, with your own happiness, that you want me to walk away, I will respect you enough to let you go.

All I have ever wanted in return is something simple: respect, appreciation, honesty and value.

Whatever happened before, I don't want to carry those chapters like weapons. I just want the things between us to be clear, peaceful and honest.

You can find love again. You can choose someone, call someone your favourite person, or make someone your whole world. That's your freedom.

And maybe that's what makes love meaningful — when someone stays because they genuinely want to, not because they are forced to.

Through everything, one thing remains: I'll always consider you my best friend forever.

Maybe our story has had confusing chapters. Maybe some things happened that neither of us understood completely.

But today, I don't want to fight the past. I just want to make the future a little more beautiful.

Always wishing the best for you,
Ahmie ❤️
