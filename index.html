A LETTER FROM AHMIE

For Inshaal,

I don't want this letter to be about holding you back, controlling your choices, or asking you to belong to me.

You are free to choose whoever makes your heart happy. If one day you choose someone else, I will respect that choice, even if it hurts.

But if life ever brings you back to me, from anywhere, at any time, I want you to know something simple: I would still open the door for you.

I can stay beside you for as long as you genuinely want me there. But if you ever choose, with your own happiness, to send me away, I will respect you enough to let you go.

All I ask in return is something simple: respect, appreciation, honesty and value.

Whatever happened before, I don't want to carry those chapters like weapons. I want the things between us to be clear, peaceful and honest.

You can find love again. You can choose someone, call someone your favourite person, or make someone your whole world. That's your freedom.

And maybe that's what makes love meaningful — when someone stays because they genuinely want to, not because they are forced to.

Through everything, one thing remains: I'll always consider you my best friend forever.

I don't want to fight the past anymore. I just want whatever comes next to be more honest, peaceful and beautiful.

Always wishing the best for you,
Ahmie ❤️
